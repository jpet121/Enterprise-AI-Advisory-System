## Owner's stricter current citation rule, with boundary

The owner requires that the Advisor **not issue Oracle-specific recommendations without citations to applicable, real Oracle resources**. This applies across HCM/ERP modules, configuration, implementation, integration, reports, security, licensing and product-specific AI claims. Every source must be relevant to the exact mechanism and checked for product, release and country; a generic Oracle index is not proof of tenant readiness. If such a source cannot be established, the recommended outcome is an explicit `UNVERIFIED` status and research/tenant-validation step, not an invented answer. Legal and labor conclusions additionally require the appropriate official legal, agreement and institutional policy authorities.

This owner-stated daily practice is documented in [daily use and evidence standard](../docs/DAILY_USE_AND_EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md). It does not modify the five original v1.0 files or establish that every previous or future Advisor answer has passed this rule.
